Market Microstructure Patterns

Order Flow Dynamics

TopicsAbsorption · Exhaustion · Icebergs · Stop runs · Delta · Imbalance · Spoofing · Vacuum
Absorption
large passive limit orders soak up aggressive flow without price moving; often precedes reversal
Exhaustion
surge of aggressive volume that fails to extend price; one side depleted
Iceberg orders
hidden size that repeatedly refills a level, creating stubborn support/resistance
Stop runs / liquidity sweeps
price spikes through a stop cluster, then reverses (stop-hunt)
Cumulative delta divergence
volume delta diverges from price, signaling weak drive
Order-book imbalance
persistent bid/ask size skew predicting near-term direction
Spoofing / layering footprints
large flickering orders pulled before execution
Liquidity vacuum / thin book
price gaps between levels when resting depth disappears

Intraday Anomalies / Recurring Behavioral Rhythms

Intraday Rhythms

TopicsORB · Opening drive · IB extension · Lunch reversal · 3 PM turn · Power hour · Gaps · Magnets
Opening range (ORB)
first 5/15/30/60 min sets initial balance; range breakouts are tradable
Opening drive vs. opening reversal
immediate continuation off the open vs. fade of the open
Initial-balance extension
breaking the first-hour range signals a developing trend day
~90% of daily highs/lows in first ~90 minutes
early session sets the extremes
Midday / lunchtime reversal
low-liquidity chop and mean reversion 11:30–13:30
Afternoon trend resumption
second directional leg after the midday lull
3:00 PM turn
frequent pivot as positioning shifts into the last hour
Power hour (15:00–16:00)
trend acceleration plus MOC imbalance drift
Closing-imbalance drift
last 10–30 min pulled by auction order flow
Gap behavior
partial gap fill (~48% same day); unfilled gap after first hour flags trend day
Reference-level magnets
prior-day high/low/close and overnight high/low act as intraday pivots
